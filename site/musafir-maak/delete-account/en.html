Delete your account

Musafir Maak app · Developer: Musafir Maak

You can delete your Musafir Maak account and its associated data at any time, in either of the two ways below.

Option 1: in the app

  • Open the Musafir Maak app and sign in.
  • Go to Profile.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.

You first need to cancel any active bookings or trips, and drivers must settle any balance owed.

Option 2: without the app

Email help@statement-ds.com with the subject "Delete account" and include the phone number registered on your account. We will confirm the request is yours (we may send a code to your number), delete the account and let you know within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account is closed immediately and can no longer be used to sign in.
  • Your public profile (name, photo, ratings) disappears from the app.
  • Your sessions, push-notification tokens and trusted devices are deleted.

What we keep, and why

We keep the following for 12 months after deletion, for legal and financial obligations, disputes and reports:

  • Trip, booking and parcel records.
  • Driver financial transactions and transfer receipts.
  • Reports and the decisions taken on them.
  • Verification documents, kept in private storage that only our review team can see.

After 12 months, your personal data (name, phone number, email, photo) and verification documents are permanently deleted. Trip and transaction records remain without anything that identifies you.
