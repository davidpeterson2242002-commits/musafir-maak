Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how the Musafir Maak app collects, uses and protects your data. The app connects travellers with car owners driving between states in Sudan, and lets people send parcels with them.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: phone number, name, gender, city, and optionally email, profile photo and WhatsApp number.
  • Verification data: a selfie and photos of your passport or national ID. For drivers: driving licence, vehicle photos, police clearance certificate and plate number.
  • Usage data: trips, bookings, parcels (description, photo, receiver name and number), ratings, reports, in-app chats and route searches.
  • Driver payment data: amounts owed and the transfer receipts drivers upload.
  • Device data: a push-notification token to send you alerts.

We do not collect your location and do not access your phone contacts.

2. Phone permissions

  • Camera: to photograph verification documents, vehicle photos and the parcel photo at pickup.
  • Photos: to choose your profile photo or a transfer receipt from your gallery.
  • Notifications: to tell you about your bookings, parcels and messages.

3. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and send verification codes.
  • To run bookings and parcels and send related notifications.
  • To verify identities, protect users and review reports.
  • To calculate driver fees and settle balances.
  • To improve the service and learn which routes are in demand.

4. Who we share it with

  • When a booking is accepted: the passenger sees the driver's name, phone number, vehicle type and plate; the driver sees the passenger's name and phone number.
  • For parcels: the receiver gets the sender's name, the driver's details and the delivery code.
  • Your public profile shows your name, photo, ratings, number of trips and join date. Your phone number is never public.
  • Service providers we use to run the app, only as needed: hosting, email, Google Firebase notifications, and WhatsApp (Meta) for verification codes and alerts.
  • Authorities, when we receive a binding legal request.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

5. Security and retention

  • Data travels between the app and our server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • ID photos and documents are kept in private storage, shown only through temporary links, and seen only by our review team.
  • Hidden phone numbers in chats are stored encrypted.
  • We keep your data while your account exists. After you delete your account we keep some of it for 12 months for legal and financial obligations and disputes, then your personal data and documents are permanently deleted.

6. Your rights

  • Edit your details from your profile page.
  • Delete your account in the app or by asking us. See the account deletion page.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or how it is used by writing to help@statement-ds.com.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will tell you about important changes in the app. For any question, email help@statement-ds.com.
